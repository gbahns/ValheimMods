Where to find her

The Bog Witch lives in a hut in the Swamp biome. Locate her by finding a Witch Stone (a special structure type that spawns in Swamp), or by exploring Swamps after the Bog Witch update is active. She sells goods for Coins, accepts certain items in trade, and unlocks new recipes once you've purchased the relevant items.
