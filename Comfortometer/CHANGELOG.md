## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-26

First release.

- A compact panel on the HUD, toggled with F4, that stays up while you move around.
- The comfort level in the title, with whether you are sheltered and how long Rested lasts at that level.
- One line per kind of comfort piece in range: its comfort points and its distance, with ×2, ×3 when more than one of the same kind is in range.
- Walk out of range of something and it stays on the list in red, with its current distance, until you are 30 m from it (Forget Beyond).
- Pieces that give nothing because a better piece of the same group is in range are dimmed, with the group named (Show Superseded, off by default).
- An unlit fire is listed in red as unlit.
- With the panel up, F4 frees the mouse to drag the panel by its title, resize it by the grip in its corner, or close it with the x; the placement is remembered. The panel fits its height to the list (Auto Height).
- Options for bold names (Bold Names) and for showing each distance against the 10 m range (Show Range).
