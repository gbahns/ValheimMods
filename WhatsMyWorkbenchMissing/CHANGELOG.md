# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-26

First release.

- Hover the level number of an open crafting station for a tooltip of the upgrades you have not built yet.
- Upgrades are split into those you could build with what you carry and those you know but lack materials for; each shows its materials, with the amounts you are short of in red as have/need.
- A count of the upgrades you have not discovered yet, without naming them (Show Undiscovered Count, on by default).
- Optionally the upgrades already built, dimmed (Show Built, off by default).
- Works for any station with extensions, vanilla or modded, since the upgrades are read from the build tools' piece tables.
